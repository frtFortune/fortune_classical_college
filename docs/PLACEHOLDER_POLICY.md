# Placeholder Policy

The website foundation is intentionally placeholder-based.

## Placeholder Rules

- Images are placeholders until approved assets are provided.
- Videos are placeholders until approved media assets are provided.
- School-specific text uses obvious placeholder text where real content is not yet available.
- No hardcoded school information should be introduced unless it has been explicitly provided by the school.
- Dynamic content should eventually come from the database rather than being embedded directly into templates.

## Intent

This policy keeps the project flexible, avoids premature assumptions, and makes it easy to replace placeholders with real content later.
